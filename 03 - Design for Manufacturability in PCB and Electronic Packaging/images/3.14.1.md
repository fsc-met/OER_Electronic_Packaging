## Suggested Content for Figure 3.14.1

### Figure title for authoring/reference only

`DRC and Broader DFM Review Coverage`

### Instructional purpose

Show why a clean EDA design-rule check is important but does not, by itself, establish complete manufacturing readiness.

The visual should distinguish:

- **configured-rule checking** performed by the PCB/EDA tool;
- the **broader DFM review scope** that adds supplier capability, assembly, access, tooling, economics, and release-data consistency.

### Central teaching message

> **DRC checks the rules that are configured and enabled. Broader DFM review asks additional process-, supplier-, access-, tooling-, and release-readiness questions that may not be encoded in the PCB editor.**

The figure should reinforce:

> **Zero DRC violations are evidence, not complete manufacturability approval.**

### Canonical PNG filename

`3.14.1 - DRC and Broader DFM Review Coverage.png`

### Intended mdBook display width

`900 px`

### Recommended canvas size / aspect ratio

- Authoring canvas: approximately `1800 x 1050 px`.
- Recommended aspect ratio: approximately `12:7` to `16:9`.
- Prefer a layered/nested review-coverage composition rather than a software screenshot.

### Overall layout

Use one large **broader DFM review** container with a clearly identifiable **EDA DRC / configured-rule checks** region inside it.

Recommended structure:

- central or left-of-center inner box:
  - `EDA DRC`
  - `Configured / enabled rules`
- inside/around that inner region, show representative encoded checks:
  - `Copper clearance`
  - `Trace width`
  - `Hole / via constraints`
  - `Solder-mask rules`
  - `Selected placement / edge rules`
- surrounding outer DFM region contains concise labeled review areas:
  - `Selected-supplier capability`
  - `Assembly process`
  - `Inspection / test access`
  - `Rework / service access`
  - `Panelization / tooling`
  - `Yield / cost tradeoffs`
  - `Release-data consistency`
- one bottom teaching statement:
  - `Zero DRC violations = checked rules pass`
  - `Not complete manufacturability approval`

Add a small qualifier near the bottom of the broader-review area:

`Scope illustration — DRC and DFM automation can overlap; the distinction here is review coverage.`

This qualifier is important because modern EDA and supplier software can automate overlapping subsets of DFM.

### Required technical relationships

- DRC must be shown as checking **configured/enabled rules**, not every conceivable manufacturing requirement.
- Broader DFM review must visibly include DRC evidence while extending beyond it.
- The figure must not imply that DRC and DFM are mutually exclusive software categories.
- The outer review areas must represent questions that may depend on:
  - selected supplier;
  - selected process;
  - physical access;
  - product intent;
  - cross-artifact consistency.
- `Zero DRC violations` should lead to a state such as `checked rules pass`, not `ready for manufacturing`.
- The visual must not imply that every broader DFM topic is always checked by a separate human; automation and engineering review can overlap.

### Spatial / geometry requirements

- Keep the DRC region visually smaller than the broader DFM coverage.
- Do not use concentric circles if they make text unreadable at 900 px; nested rounded rectangles or a central DRC panel surrounded by clearly separated topic boxes are preferred.
- Keep all broader-review categories inside one common DFM boundary so the visual reads as one integrated review scope. The DRC box should be visibly embedded within that broader scope rather than drawn as a disconnected competing method.
- Keep the bottom `zero DRC` teaching statement visually separated from the review-category labels.
- Avoid arrows that suggest a strict chronological process; this figure is about **coverage/scope**, not workflow.
- If arrows are used, use them only to show the logical relation from `zero DRC violations` to `evidence, not complete approval`.


### Color / style guidance

- Clean engineering-textbook appearance consistent with the current EPAC figure set.
- Explicit white background; never rely on viewer transparency/default canvas color.
- Flat 2D vector/schematic style rather than photorealistic software screenshots.
- Restrained engineering palette:
  - blue/blue-gray for normal review/check paths;
  - neutral gray for controlled documents, review frames, and secondary objects;
  - muted green for acceptable/closed/consistent states;
  - muted orange for caution or engineering-review states;
  - limited red only for mismatches, unresolved findings, or blocked release states.
- Sans-serif labels sized for the 900 px mdBook render.
- Moderate line weights and consistent visual hierarchy.
- Do not rely on color alone; pair color with labels, arrows, line style, symbols, or panel structure.
- No decorative gradients, glossy rendering, vendor logos, software UI chrome, watermarks, or branded screenshots.
- Do not embed the external figure number, final caption, or an oversized duplicate overall title inside the artwork.
