## Suggested Content for Figure 3.7.3

**Figure title for authoring/reference only:**  
`Via Geometry and Aspect-Ratio Tradeoff`

### Instructional purpose

Show why via drill diameter must be reviewed together with board thickness, and reinforce that a through via is a three-dimensional plated structure interacting with connected and nonconnected copper layers.

The figure should make two ideas visible at once because they belong to the same via-geometry teaching role:

- aspect ratio increases when drill diameter decreases at the same board thickness;
- the via barrel connects selected copper layers while requiring clearance/antipad through nonconnected copper.

The figure should support the section's numerical examples:

- `1.6 mm / 0.20 mm = 8:1`;
- `1.6 mm / 0.15 mm ≈ 10.7:1`.

### Central teaching message

> **A smaller via drill is not just a smaller hole. At the same board thickness it creates a higher-aspect-ratio plated structure with less manufacturing margin.**

Secondary message:

> **A through via connects only the intended copper features; nonconnected planes require clearance around the barrel/pad.**

### Required technical relationships

The final figure must correctly show that:

1. Both panels use the same `1.6 mm` board thickness.
2. The right-panel drill diameter is smaller than the left-panel drill diameter.
3. `1.6 / 0.20 = 8`.
4. `1.6 / 0.15 ≈ 10.7`.
5. The aspect-ratio denominator is the production drill/hole diameter used by the applicable fabricator capability definition, consistent with the section.
6. The plated barrel connects top/bottom pads and the intentionally connected internal copper.
7. The nonconnected plane does **not** touch the via barrel/pad; an antipad/clearance surrounds it.
8. The figure must not imply that every internal layer is connected.
9. The figure must not imply that 8:1 is a universal maximum or that 10.7:1 is universally unacceptable.
10. The figure must not introduce blind, buried, stacked, or laser microvia architectures.

### Technical-verification basis

#### Current Section 3.7

The section establishes:

- `AR = T_b / D_d`;
- `T_b = 1.6 mm`;
- `D_d = 0.20 mm → AR = 8:1`;
- `D_d = 0.15 mm → AR ≈ 10.7:1`;
- `0.20-0.30 mm` as a useful routine mechanical-via drill scale;
- `0.15 mm` as available but tighter/process-dependent;
- a through via includes barrel, pads, annular ring, and clearances/antipads on nonconnected layers.

#### Current technical verification already recorded in the section

The section states that:

- mechanical drill capability can reach `0.15 mm` in some services;
- values below about `0.20 mm` can require tighter/extra-cost processing;
- current published standard aspect-ratio guidance includes `1:8`;
- near `10:1` and above is more process dependent.

The figure must present these values as engineering-scale examples, not universal fab limits.
