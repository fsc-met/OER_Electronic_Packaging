## Suggested Content for Figure 3.13.3

### Figure title for authoring/reference only

`Global and Local Fiducials for Assembly Alignment`

### Instructional purpose

Teach the difference between:

- **global/panel fiducials**, which establish the position and rotation of the panel or board coordinate frame;
- **local fiducials**, which can provide additional registration near an individual PCB image or precision-placement region.

The figure should also show the basic fiducial-target concept: an exposed circular optical target with a clear surrounding area.

### Central teaching message

> **Global and local fiducials reference different spatial scales. The machine must be able to see a clean optical target and use the appropriate coordinate frame for the required placement accuracy.**

### Canonical PNG filename

`3.13.3 - Global and Local Fiducials for Assembly Alignment.png`

### Intended mdBook display width

`900 px`

### Recommended canvas size / aspect ratio

- Authoring canvas: approximately `1800 x 1050 px`.
- Recommended aspect ratio: approximately `12:7` to `16:9`.
- Use one main panel-level top view plus one enlarged fiducial detail inset.

### Overall layout

Main view:
- rectangular panel with repeated PCB images;
- three widely separated panel/global fiducials arranged asymmetrically;
- one or two local fiducials near an individual board or fine-pitch component region;
- simple dashed coordinate/registration cues.

Inset:
- enlarged circular fiducial target;
- exposed copper center;
- larger clear surrounding region;
- optional solder-mask opening boundary if shown carefully.

### Required objects and labels

Main-view labels:

- `Global / panel fiducial`
- `Local fiducial`
- `Panel coordinate frame`
- `Local precision region`

Inset labels:

- `Exposed copper target`
- `Clear surrounding area`

Optional:
- `Board image`
- `Fine-pitch placement region`

Do not embed a universal numeric dimension inside the inset.

### Required technical relationships

- Global fiducials should be widely separated and asymmetric enough to support unambiguous position/rotation reference.
- Local fiducial should be physically associated with one PCB image or a local precision region, not placed randomly.
- Global and local fiducials must use the same basic optical-target visual language while being distinguished by location and role.
- Fiducials must be surface targets, not through-holes, and must remain visually distinct from the mechanical tooling holes shown elsewhere in the section.
- Clear area must surround the optical target.
- Do not imply that local fiducials are required for every component or every PCB.
- Do not imply that three is a universal minimum for all machines; present the three-global example as a common panel-level practice.

### Spatial / geometry requirements

- Place global fiducials near panel corners/rails but with enough inset to avoid clipping and edge ambiguity.
- Avoid a perfectly symmetric triangle that could encourage 180-degree ambiguity.
- Keep local fiducials clearly inside or adjacent to the local board/precision region.
- Use dashed axis arrows or coordinate-frame indicators sparingly and ensure they reference the appropriate panel/global or local frame rather than forming decorative crosshairs.
- In the inset, make the copper target visibly smaller than the clear surrounding area.
- Ensure fiducial inset does not visually resemble a tooling-hole section.
