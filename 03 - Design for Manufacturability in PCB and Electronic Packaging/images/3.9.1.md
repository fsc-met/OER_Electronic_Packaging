# Figure 3.9.1 Suggested Content Specification

## Figure title for authoring/reference only
**Solder-Mask Expansion and Registration Margin**

## Instructional purpose
Show how copper-pad size, nominal solder-mask opening, positive expansion, and finite mask registration interact.

## Central teaching message
A nominally centered opening does not guarantee equal realized clearance on every side after fabrication. Positive expansion provides nominal margin, but registration shift can reduce clearance on one side.

## Canonical PNG filename
`3.9.1 - Solder-Mask Expansion and Registration Margin.png`

## Intended mdBook display width
`900`

## Recommended canvas size / aspect ratio
- Recommended: **2000 × 1200 px**, approximately **5:3**.
- Equivalent aspect ratio is acceptable if spacing improves.

## Overall layout
Use a **two-panel matched top-view comparison**:

1. **Nominal centered opening**
2. **Same opening after representative registration shift**

Keep pad size and opening size identical between panels.

## Required objects and labels
- copper pad;
- solder-mask field;
- mask opening;
- pad width/diameter;
- opening width/diameter;
- positive expansion per side;
- registration-shift arrow;
- reduced realized clearance on shifted side.

Required labels:
- `Copper pad`
- `Solder-mask opening`
- `Positive expansion, E`
- `Nominal centered opening`
- `Representative registration shift`
- `Reduced realized clearance`

Optional approved example:
- pad = **0.30 mm**
- expansion = **0.05 mm/side**
- opening = **0.40 mm**

## Required technical relationships
Use:
$$
E=\frac{W_{\text{open}}-W_{\text{pad}}}{2}
$$

and:
$$
W_{\text{open}}=W_{\text{pad}}+2E
$$

Approved example:
$$
0.30+2(0.05)=0.40\ \text{mm}
$$

The right panel changes **registration only**; copper geometry and nominal opening size remain unchanged.

Do not imply:
- deterministic failure;
- a universal required expansion;
- that every opening must be larger than the pad.

## Spatial / geometry requirements
- Top view only.
- Matched scale across panels.
- Expansion dimension runs from copper edge to nominal opening edge.
- Shift arrow represents mask movement relative to copper.
- Avoid leader crossings and crowded labels.

## Color / style guidance

- Muted copper/orange for copper.
- Muted green for solder mask.
- White/light neutral opening.
- Dark gray/blue-gray dimensions and leaders.
- Flat textbook style; no gradients, shadows, decorative icons, or poster effects.

## Scope boundaries

## Technical-verification basis
Current Section 3.9 and approved outline establish:
- finite mask registration;
- conventional positive expansion around **0.05–0.10 mm/side** as a useful planning scale;
- some current direct-imaging processes can support tighter or nominal 1:1 openings;
- SMD geometry may intentionally use opening smaller than copper.
