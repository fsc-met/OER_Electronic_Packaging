# Figure 3.9.2 Suggested Content Specification

## Figure title for authoring/reference only
**Fine-Pitch Pads and Solder-Mask Web**

## Instructional purpose
Show how pitch, copper-pad width, and mask expansion determine the remaining solder-mask web between adjacent fine-pitch openings.

## Central teaching message
Larger pad width, larger opening expansion, or tighter pitch reduces the remaining mask web. If the web becomes too small for the selected process, separate mask dams may not survive.

## Canonical PNG filename
`3.9.2 - Fine-Pitch Pads and Solder-Mask Web.png`

## Intended mdBook display width
`900`

## Recommended canvas size / aspect ratio
- Recommended: **2000 × 1200 px**, approximately **5:3**.

## Overall layout
Use a **two-panel matched top-view comparison**:

1. **Web maintained**
2. **Web becomes process-sensitive / openings may merge**

Keep pitch and copper-pad width identical between panels; change only mask expansion.

## Required objects and labels
- two equal pads per panel;
- mask openings around both pads;
- center-to-center pitch dimension;
- pad-width dimension;
- expansion dimension;
- remaining mask-web dimension.

Required labels:
- `Pitch, p`
- `Pad width, w`
- `Mask expansion, E`
- `Mask web, B`
- `Web maintained`
- `Web becomes process-sensitive` or `Openings may merge`

Approved examples:
- `p = 0.50 mm`
- `w = 0.30 mm`
- left: `E = 0.05 mm`, `B = 0.10 mm`
- right: `E = 0.075 mm`, `B = 0.05 mm`

## Required technical relationships
Use:
$$
B=p-w-2E
$$

Checks:
$$
0.50-0.30-2(0.05)=0.10\ \text{mm}
$$

$$
0.50-0.30-2(0.075)=0.05\ \text{mm}
$$

The web is measured **opening edge to opening edge**, not copper-pad edge to copper-pad edge.

Do not imply that 0.05 mm is universally impossible; label it process-sensitive.

## Spatial / geometry requirements
- Top view only.
- Pads colinear and equal.
- Pitch dimension center-to-center.
- Pad width edge-to-edge.
- Expansion pad-edge to opening-edge.
- Web opening-edge to opening-edge.
- Same scale in both panels.

## Scope boundaries

## Technical-verification basis
Current Section 3.9 retains **0.10 mm** as a useful mainstream teaching reference while noting current DI/LDI processes may support tighter values around **0.07–0.075 mm** and other processes/colors/copper thicknesses may require more.
