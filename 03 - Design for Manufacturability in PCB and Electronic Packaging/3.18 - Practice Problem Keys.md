### Problem 12 - Nominal Annular Ring

1. **0.20 mm**
2. **Yes**
3. **Yes**
4. **0.90 mm**

For the first feature:

$$
A_r=\frac{0.80-0.40}{2}=0.20\ \mathrm{mm}
$$

For the second feature:

$$
D_p=D_h+2A_r
$$

$$
D_p=0.50+2(0.20)=0.90\ \mathrm{mm}
$$

These are simplified nominal centered-hole calculations using finished-hole diameter.
