# 3.17 - Practice Problems

These problems review the major concepts developed throughout Chapter 3.

Each problem has **one deterministic answer or answer set** based on the Chapter 3 material and any data supplied in the problem. Problems are grouped in the same general sequence as the chapter. Numerical problems use **supplied exercise rules or capability data** rather than assuming that one universal DFM value applies to every product or supplier.

The answer key is provided in **Section 3.18**.

Most problems are short applied checks. Use the Chapter 3 figures and tables when useful, but every exercise that depends on a supplier- or product-specific numerical rule supplies the rule needed for a deterministic answer.

### Problem 12 - Nominal Annular Ring

For this simplified **nominal centered-hole** screening calculation, use the **finished hole diameter**:

$$
A_r=\frac{D_p-D_h}{2}
$$

where:

- $A_r$ = nominal annular ring;
- $D_p$ = pad diameter;
- $D_h$ = finished hole diameter.

A pad diameter is **0.80 mm** and the finished hole diameter is **0.40 mm**.

The supplied review values are:

- minimum acceptable nominal annular ring: **0.15 mm**;
- preferred nominal annular ring: **0.20 mm**.

1. Calculate the nominal annular ring.
2. Does it meet the minimum?
3. Does it meet the preferred value?
4. A second plated hole requires a **0.50 mm finished hole** and a **0.20 mm preferred nominal annular ring**. Using the same nominal centered-hole relationship, what minimum pad diameter is required?

### Deterministic-problem rule

Every published problem in this section must have:

- one unambiguous answer or bounded answer set;
- sufficient information in the question or Chapter 3 text;
- a matching answer in Section 3.18;
- no grading dependence on open-ended design judgment;
- supplied governing rules/capability data whenever a numerical DFM decision would otherwise depend on supplier/product context.
