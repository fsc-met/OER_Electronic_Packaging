## Suggested Content for Figure 2.6.2

**Figure title for authoring/reference only:**  
`From Drilled Hole to Finished Plated Through-Hole`

**Instructional purpose:**  
Provide a simple geometry-focused comparison that supports Section 2.6.3 by showing why a **finished plated hole is smaller than the opening immediately after drilling**.

The figure should reinforce the idealized relationship:

$$
D_f \approx D_d - 2t_p
$$

where:

- $D_f$ = finished hole diameter;
- $D_d$ = drilled-hole diameter before wall metallization;
- $t_p$ = effective metal buildup on one side of the hole wall.

The figure should answer:

> **What physically changes between a drilled hole and the finished plated through-hole?**

The central teaching point is:

> **The hole wall is prepared and metallized, creating a conductive barrel that reduces the open diameter and connects to annular rings / conductive layers.**

**Filename:**  
`2.6.2 - From Drilled Hole to Finished Plated Through-Hole.png`

**Intended width:** 900 px in Markdown

**Recommended aspect ratio:** approximately 16:9

# Left Panel - Drilled Hole Before Metallization

Show a simple multilayer PCB cross-section with a drilled opening.

Required features:

- exposed dielectric/glass-resin hole wall;
- exposed inner-layer copper edges where the drill passes through selected layers;
- no continuous copper barrel yet;
- top and bottom copper pads / lands may be shown.

Required label:

- `Drilled-hole diameter, Dd`

Recommended label:

- `Drilled / prepared hole wall`

If a hole-preparation cue is used, keep it generic.

Do not show:

- electroless-copper chemistry;
- electroplating tanks;
- desmear chemistry;
- detailed drill defects.

Those topics belong in Section 2.8 or 2.12.

---

# Right Panel - Finished Plated Through-Hole

Show the same hole after wall metallization/plating.

Required features:

- continuous copper-colored barrel on the hole wall;
- visibly smaller open diameter than in the left panel;
- annular ring / pad at one or both outer surfaces;
- intentional connection to at least one selected inner copper layer.

Required labels:

- `Finished hole diameter, Df`
- `Wall plating thickness, tp`
- `Plated barrel`
- `Annular ring`

The wall-plating thickness label should point to **one side** of the barrel.

The geometry must visually support:

$$
D_f \approx D_d - 2t_p
$$

without implying perfect process uniformity.

# Required Technical Relationships

The figure must communicate all of the following:

- drilling creates the initial opening;
- the hole wall is not yet a finished conductive barrel immediately after drilling;
- later metallization/plating adds conductive metal to the wall;
- wall buildup reduces the open diameter;
- the finished hole is therefore smaller than the pre-plating drilled opening in this simplified geometry;
- annular rings connect the barrel to copper features on selected layers;
- actual finished-hole size also depends on process tolerances;
- the displayed equation is conceptual/idealized and is not a universal drill-compensation rule.
