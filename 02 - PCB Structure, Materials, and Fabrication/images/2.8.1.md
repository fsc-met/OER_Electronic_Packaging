## Suggested Content for Figure 2.8.1

**Figure title for authoring/reference only:**  
`Overall Rigid Multilayer PCB Fabrication Flow`

**Instructional purpose:**  
Provide the visual mental map for the entire Section 2.8 process.

The figure should help MET readers recognize the fabrication chain as **five physical transformations** rather than memorize a long list of disconnected machines:

1. create inner copper layers;
2. build and laminate the multilayer structure;
3. create and metallize holes;
4. create and protect outer circuitry;
5. profile and verify the finished bare board.

The central teaching message is:

> **PCB fabrication is a cumulative sequence in which each operation changes the physical board and prepares it for the next operation.**

**Canonical filename:**  
`2.8.1 - Overall Rigid Multilayer PCB Fabrication Flow.png`

**Intended public width:** 900 px

**Recommended authoring canvas:** approximately `1800 × 1000` px unless the final composition clearly benefits from a modestly different aspect ratio.

**Recommended aspect ratio:** approximately 16:9 to 9:5, with enough vertical room for labels and safe margins.

# Overall Layout

Use a clean **left-to-right five-phase process flow** across the page.

Recommended composition:

- five large phase blocks;
- each phase contains a small simplified engineering sketch plus 2–4 concise operation labels;
- arrows connect the five phase blocks;

Recommended phase labels:

2. `Build multilayer structure`

5. `Profile and verify`

Within the phases, show only the most recognizable operation labels:

- clean / image / etch / inspect;

- drill / desmear / metallize;
- outer image / plate / etch / solder mask / finish / legend;
- profile / electrical test / final inspection.

# Required Technical Relationships

- The starting condition is copper-clad laminate/core material, not a populated PCB.
- Inner-layer copper patterning occurs before multilayer lamination.
- Drilling is shown before hole metallization.
- The drilled hole must not be shown as electrically conductive until after metallization/plating.
- Outer-layer creation must visibly include copper build-up/plating before the final outer etch in the representative pattern-plating route.
- Solder mask and surface finish must remain distinct functions.
- The process must end at a verified bare PCB or assembly-ready bare-board array, not at a PCBA.
- Exact factory routing is representative and may vary by fabricator/technology.
