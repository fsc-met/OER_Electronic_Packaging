## Suggested Content for Figure 2.8.4

**Figure title for authoring/reference only:**  
`From Drilled Hole to Metallized Through-Hole`

**Instructional purpose:**  
Show that vertical interconnection is created in **multiple manufacturing stages**, not by drilling alone.

The central sequence is:

> **drill -> desmear / clean -> initial conductive metallization -> later copper build-up**

The figure must reinforce Section 2.6 without duplicating its dimensional hole/via anatomy figures.

**Canonical filename:**  
`2.8.4 - From Drilled Hole to Metallized Through-Hole.png`

**Intended public width:** 900 px

**Recommended authoring canvas:** approximately `1800 × 1000` px unless the final composition clearly benefits from a modestly different aspect ratio.

**Recommended aspect ratio:** approximately 16:9 to 9:5, with enough vertical room for labels and safe margins.

---

# Overall Layout

Use a **four-state cross-sectional sequence** through the same multilayer board.

Recommended states:

1. `After drilling`
2. `After desmear / hole preparation`
3. `Initial conductive layer`
4. `Copper barrel built up`

State 1:
- show drilled wall with subtle resin-smear indication over at least one inner-layer copper edge.

State 2:
- remove smear and expose clean inner-layer copper edges.

State 3:
- show a very thin continuous conductive/electroless copper coating on the hole wall.

State 4:
- show thicker electroplated copper barrel connected to selected inner-layer copper features.

Keep the hole open; do not show filled/capped via structures.

Do not place the external figure number, final public caption, revision notes, or a large decorative title inside the artwork.

The artwork should remain readable when reduced to the 900 px mdBook display width.

---

# Required Technical Relationships

- Drilling creates geometry but not electrical connectivity.
- Smear should be shown as resin residue associated with drilling, not as random contamination everywhere.
- Desmear must expose the intended inner-layer copper connection surface.
- Initial metallization must be thin and continuous on the otherwise nonconductive wall.
- The final barrel build-up must be visibly thicker than the initial conductive layer.
- The plated barrel must connect only to intended inner-layer copper features; nonconnected layers should show clearances.
- Do not imply one universal electroless chemistry; direct metallization is an acknowledged alternative.
