## Suggested Content for Figure 2.5.4

**Figure title for authoring/reference only:**  
`Solid Plane Connection versus Thermal-Relief Connection`

**Instructional purpose:**  
Provide one direct comparison figure that supports Section 2.5.6 by showing why a pad or plated hole connected **solidly** to a large copper region behaves differently during soldering from one connected through a **thermal relief**.

The figure should reinforce the Section 2.5.6 tradeoff:

> **A solid connection provides a more direct electrical and thermal path to the plane, while a thermal relief preserves electrical continuity through narrow spokes but restricts lateral heat flow during soldering.**

The figure should answer:

> **How does the copper geometry of a thermal relief reduce heat sinking compared with a solid plane connection?**

The figure should also make clear that:

> **A thermal relief is not a thermal via.**
