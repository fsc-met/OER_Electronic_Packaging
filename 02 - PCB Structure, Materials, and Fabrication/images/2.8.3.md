## Suggested Content for Figure 2.8.3

**Figure title for authoring/reference only:**  
`Multilayer Layup and Lamination`

**Instructional purpose:**  
Show the physical difference between **layup** and **lamination**.

The figure should teach:

- layup = arranging and registering the stack materials;
- lamination = heat + pressure causing prepreg resin flow and cure so the stack becomes one rigid bonded panel.

The central transformation is:

> **separate patterned cores + prepreg + outer copper foil -> one bonded multilayer panel**

**Canonical filename:**  
`2.8.3 - Multilayer Layup and Lamination.png`

**Intended public width:** 900 px

**Recommended authoring canvas:** approximately `1800 × 1000` px unless the final composition clearly benefits from a modestly different aspect ratio.

**Recommended aspect ratio:** approximately 16:9 to 9:5, with enough vertical room for labels and safe margins.

---

# Overall Layout

Use a **before / during / after cross-sectional sequence**.

Recommended states:

### Left - Layup
Show separated or slightly spaced layers in correct order:

- outer copper foil;
- prepreg;
- patterned inner-layer core;
- prepreg;
- patterned inner-layer core;
- prepreg;
- outer copper foil.

Use alignment/registration cues.

### Center - Lamination
Show:

- compression plates or pressure arrows from top/bottom;
- heat symbols/arrows;
- prepreg resin flowing around copper relief;
- stack thickness reducing slightly as layers consolidate.

### Right - Bonded multilayer panel
Show:

- one rigid consolidated panel;
- copper layers embedded within dielectric;
- outer copper foil bonded to the surface.

A small note may state `resin flows, fills gaps, cures, and bonds layers`.

Do not place the external figure number, final public caption, revision notes, or a large decorative title inside the artwork.

The artwork should remain readable when reduced to the 900 px mdBook display width.

# Required Technical Relationships

- Patterned inner layers must already exist before layup.
- Prepreg must be visually distinct from fully cured core dielectric.
- Outer copper foil should remain unpatterned at this stage in the representative route.
- Heat and pressure must be shown during lamination, not during layup.
- Resin flow should fill spaces around etched inner copper rather than leave obvious large voids.
- Cure must be represented as the transformation from partially cured prepreg resin to bonded rigid dielectric.
- Residual stress may be mentioned conceptually, but the figure should not become a warpage-mechanics diagram.
