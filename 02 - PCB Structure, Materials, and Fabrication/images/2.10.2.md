## Suggested Content for Figure 2.10.2

**Figure title for authoring/reference only:**  
`Balanced and Unbalanced PCB Stack-Ups Produce Different Warpage Tendencies`

**Instructional purpose:**  
Show why **stack-up symmetry** and **copper balance** matter to PCB flatness.

The figure should help MET readers understand four linked ideas:

1. a PCB is a through-thickness layered structure with a meaningful **mid-plane**;
2. thermal exposure and cool-down can create internal thermomechanical stress;
3. a reasonably balanced construction can allow opposing effects to partially cancel;
4. a strongly unbalanced construction is more likely to create a **net bending moment** and visible warpage.

The central teaching message is:

> **Differential thermal strain becomes visible warpage when mechanical constraint acts through an unbalanced through-thickness construction.**

**Canonical filename:**  
`2.10.2 - Balanced and Unbalanced PCB Stack-Ups Produce Different Warpage Tendencies.png`

**Intended public width:** 900 px

**Recommended authoring canvas:** approximately `1800 × 1000` px unless a modestly different canvas improves clarity.

**Recommended aspect ratio:** approximately 16:9 or 5:3.

---

# Overall Layout

Use a clean **two-column comparison figure**.

Recommended composition:

- **Left column:** a `Reasonably balanced stack-up`.
- **Right column:** a `Strongly unbalanced stack-up`.

Within each column, show a simple top-to-bottom sequence:

1. a cross-section of the stack-up with copper and dielectric layers;
2. the board **mid-plane** identified;
3. thermal-exposure / cool-down arrows or a short process cue;
4. a resulting board shape below the cross-section.

The balanced side should end with a nearly flat board or much smaller curvature.

The unbalanced side should end with clearly larger curvature.

A small intermediate annotation such as `internal stresses partly cancel` on the balanced side and `net bending moment remains` on the unbalanced side is recommended.

Do not place the external figure number, final public caption, revision notes, or a large decorative title inside the artwork.

The figure should remain legible when reduced to the 900 px mdBook display width.

---

# Required Technical Relationships

- The board mid-plane must be explicitly shown.
- The balanced and unbalanced examples must be recognizably different through the thickness.
- The balanced example should show approximate symmetry in dielectric/copper distribution about the mid-plane.
- The unbalanced example should show a clear asymmetry, such as:
  - heavier copper on one side;
  - noticeably different dielectric distribution;
  - a one-sided stiffness/material imbalance.
- The figure must connect **thermal exposure and cool-down** to internal stress and residual deformation tendency.
- The balanced example should still allow internal stress to exist; it must **not** imply zero stress or guaranteed perfect flatness.
- The unbalanced example should show the idea of a remaining **net bending moment** or remaining imbalance.
- The figure must support the public text statement that global warpage depends on:
  - differential thermomechanical strain;
  - constraint;
  - through-thickness imbalance.
- The figure must not imply that Z-axis CTE mismatch alone fully explains global board curvature.
