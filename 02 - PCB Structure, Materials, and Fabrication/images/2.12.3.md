# Figure 2.12.3 Suggested Content

## Figure title for authoring/reference only
**Figure 2.12.3 - Common Laminate and Interface Defects in a Bare PCB**

## Instructional purpose
Help MET students distinguish several commonly confused **laminate and interface defect modes** and understand where each defect occurs physically.

## Central teaching message
Laminate/interface defects are not one generic class:
- **delamination** = separation between bonded layers;
- **blistering** = localized raised/swollen separation;
- **measling/crazing** = internal glass-resin disturbance visible as white features;
- **lifted land** = local copper-to-laminate adhesion failure.

If measling and crazing must share one panel for layout economy, clearly differentiate them within that panel.

## Required objects and labels
Required labels:
- `Delamination`
- `Blistering`
- `Measling`
- `Crazing`
- `Lifted land`

Required visual elements:
- multilayer board cross-section or partial section;
- bonded layers/interfaces;
- visible separation gap for delamination;
- local dome/swelling for blistering;
- white internal speckled or crossed features for measling;
- more connected white internal pattern for crazing;
- copper pad/land peeling away from the laminate surface for lifted land.

Optional clarifying callouts:
- `Layer separation`
- `Localized raised area`
- `Internal glass-resin disturbance`
- `Copper-to-laminate adhesion failure`

## Required technical relationships
- Delamination must appear as a true interfacial separation.
- Blistering must be shown as a localized swollen manifestation of separation, not merely a color change.
- Measling and crazing must be shown **inside the laminate**, not on the copper surface.
- Crazing should look more connected or line-like than measling.
- The lifted land must specifically show copper separation from the supporting surface laminate.
- Keep all representations conceptual but faithful.

## Spatial / geometry requirements
- Use enough magnification so subtle differences between measling and crazing remain visible at 900 px.
- Avoid overcrowding with too many explanatory notes.
- Keep callout leaders short and unambiguous.
- Ensure lifted-land geometry is clearly visible in profile.

## Color / style guidance
- White background.
- Neutral laminate colors.
- Copper in warm copper tone.
- White or pale internal disturbance marks for measling/crazing.
- Dark neutral label text.
- Avoid realistic textures or excessive shading.
