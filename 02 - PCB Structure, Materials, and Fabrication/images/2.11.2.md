## Suggested Content for Figure 2.11.2

**Figure title for authoring/reference only:**  
`Copper Planes and Thermal Vias Create a PCB Heat Path`

**Instructional purpose:**  
Show how a practical PCB heat path combines heat collection, vertical conduction, and lateral spreading.

**Central teaching message:**

> **Thermal lands, plated via barrels, and continuous copper planes work together; no single feature is the entire thermal solution.**

**Canonical PNG filename:**  
`2.11.2 - Copper Planes and Thermal Vias Create a PCB Heat Path.png`

**Intended mdBook display width:** 900 px

**Recommended canvas:** approximately `1800 × 1050` px

---

# Overall Layout

Use a single simplified PCB cross-section centered around a heat-generating component.

Recommended heat path:

`Hot component -> top thermal land -> thermal via field -> internal copper plane / lower copper region -> lateral heat spreading`

Use arrows to show the intended path.

A compact side callout may identify that dielectric regions remain higher-resistance paths.

---

# Required Objects and Labels

Required:

- hot surface-mounted component;
- exposed thermal pad or thermal land beneath/near the component;
- multilayer PCB cross-section;
- several plated thermal vias;
- at least one continuous internal copper plane;
- lower copper land or larger heat-spreading region;
- heat-flow arrows;
- labels:
  - `Hot component`;
  - `Thermal land`;
  - `Thermal vias`;
  - `Copper plane`;
  - `Lateral heat spreading`;
  - `Dielectric / laminate`.

Optional:

- `Many vertical paths in parallel`;
- `Low-k dielectric around copper path`.

---

# Required Technical Relationships

- Heat must first reach the thermal land.
- Thermal vias must connect to useful copper.
- Internal plane should spread heat laterally.
- The figure must show that via barrels are copper paths, not solid copper rods unless explicitly labeled as filled vias.
- Do not imply that every via in a PCB is automatically a thermal via.
- Do not imply that the plane itself rejects heat to ambient without another downstream path.

---

# Spatial / Geometry Requirements

- Make the via barrels clearly hollow/plated or otherwise visually recognizable as plated holes.
- Avoid so many vias that the artwork becomes a via-field poster.
- Four to eight vias are enough for the concept.
- Keep copper layers thin but visible.
- Use a consistent cross-section scale.
- Keep heat arrows clear of labels.
- Preserve safe margins.
