## Suggested Content for Figure 2.8.6

**Figure title for authoring/reference only:**  
`From Outer Circuitry to Protected and Marked PCB Surface`

**Instructional purpose:**  
Give the **late-stage surface-completion operations** the same visual weight as the earlier manufacturing stages.

The figure should show three distinct physical functions:

1. solder mask protects copper that should remain covered;
2. surface finish protects intentionally exposed conductive surfaces;
3. legend/marking adds readable manufacturing/assembly information without changing circuit connectivity.

The central message is:

> **completed outer circuitry is not yet a finished board surface. It must be protected, finished, and marked.**

**Canonical filename:**  
`2.8.6 - From Outer Circuitry to Protected and Marked PCB Surface.png`

**Intended public width:** 900 px

**Recommended authoring canvas:** approximately `1800 × 1000` px unless the final composition clearly benefits from a modestly different aspect ratio.

**Recommended aspect ratio:** approximately 16:9 to 9:5, with enough vertical room for labels and safe margins.

---

# Overall Layout

Use a **four-state top-view plus small cross-section sequence**.

Recommended states:

1. `Completed outer circuitry`
   - exposed copper traces/pads.

2. `Solder mask applied and imaged`
   - mask covers traces;
   - mask openings remain over pads/test/contact features.

3. `Surface finish applied`
   - only the intentionally exposed conductive features receive the finish.

4. `Legend / marking added`
   - reference designators, polarity/orientation, board ID/revision or other concise sample markings;
   - no marking over pads/test points/fiducials.

A small cross-section inset may be used in States 2–3 to make mask-versus-finish coverage unmistakable, but do not duplicate the full Figure 2.7.1 composition.

Do not place the external figure number, final public caption, revision notes, or a large decorative title inside the artwork.

The artwork should remain readable when reduced to the 900 px mdBook display width.

---

# Required Technical Relationships

- Solder mask and surface finish must remain visually distinct.
- Solder mask covers most outer copper while leaving intended openings.
- Surface finish is applied only to intentionally exposed conductive surfaces.
- Legend is nonconductive marking and must not be drawn as part of the copper stack.
- Legend must not cover functional pads, test points, contacts, holes, or fiducials.
- The figure must remain finish-type-neutral; do not choose ENIG/HASL/OSP as the universal finish.
- The representative sequence may show finish before legend, but a note may state that exact finish/marking order varies by fabricator/process.

---

# Minimum Labels

Required or strongly recommended labels:

- `Completed outer circuitry`
- `Solder mask`
- `Mask opening`
- `Surface finish`
- `Legend / marking`
- `Exposed functional pad`
- `Protected copper`

Keep labels concise. Prefer short engineering terms over explanatory sentences.
