### Problem 18 - Compare First-Order PCB Bending Rigidity

Two simplified PCB strips have the same:

- width;
- length;
- effective elastic modulus;
- support condition.

Only thickness differs:

- Board A: $t_A=1.0\ \mathrm{mm}$;
- Board B: $t_B=1.5\ \mathrm{mm}$.

For the first-order rectangular-section comparison,

$$
I=\frac{bt^3}{12}
$$

and therefore, for equal width and modulus,

$$
\frac{EI_B}{EI_A}=
\left(\frac{t_B}{t_A}\right)^3
$$

1. Calculate $EI_B/EI_A$ and report the ratio to three significant digits.
2. If deflection for the comparison load case is inversely proportional to $EI$, calculate $\delta_B/\delta_A$ and report it to three significant digits.
3. Mark **True (T)** or **False (F)**:  
   This calculation is a complete structural model of a real multilayer PCB.

### Problem 20 - One-Dimensional PCB Heat-Conduction Estimate

A simplified dielectric region has:

- thickness: $L=0.25\ \mathrm{mm}=2.5\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{m}$;
- heat-flow area: $A=12\ \mathrm{mm}\times10\ \mathrm{mm}=1.2\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{m^2}$;
- thermal conductivity: $k=0.25\ \mathrm{W/(m\cdot K)}$;
- heat flow: $Q=1.5\ \mathrm{W}$.

$$
R_{\mathrm{cond}}=\frac{L}{kA}

$$
\Delta T=Q R_{\mathrm{cond}}

2. Calculate the temperature-rise magnitude $\Delta T$ in $^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and report it to three significant digits.
