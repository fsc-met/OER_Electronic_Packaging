## Part C - Mechanical and Thermal Behavior, and Bare-Board Defects

### Problem 18 - Compare First-Order PCB Bending Rigidity

1. **$EI_B/EI_A=3.38$**

$$
\frac{EI_B}{EI_A}
=
\left(\frac{t_B}{t_A}\right)^3
=
\left(\frac{1.5}{1.0}\right)^3
=
3.375
\approx
3.38
$$

2. **$\delta_B/\delta_A=0.296$**

For this comparison,

$$
\delta\propto\frac{1}{EI}
$$

so

$$
\frac{\delta_B}{\delta_A}
=
\frac{EI_A}{EI_B}
=
\frac{1}{3.375}
=
0.296
$$

3. **F**

This is a first-order comparison. A real multilayer PCB is a layered anisotropic structure whose response also depends on materials, copper distribution, geometry, support conditions, residual stress, and other factors.

---

### Problem 19 - Bow, Twist, and Warpage Control

**Answer:** `1-B, 2-T, 3-C, 4-C, 5-C`

Increasing thickness can reduce deformation under a given loading condition, but it does not automatically remove an imbalance that is driving warpage.

### Problem 20 - One-Dimensional PCB Heat-Conduction Estimate

1. **$R_{\mathrm{cond}}=8.33\ \mathrm{K/W}$**

$$
R_{\mathrm{cond}}
=
\frac{L}{kA}
=
\frac{2.5\times10^{-4}}
{(0.25)(1.2\times10^{-4})}
=
8.33\ \mathrm{K/W}
$$

2. **$\Delta T=12.5^\circ\mathrm{C}$**

$$
\Delta T
=
Q R_{\mathrm{cond}}
=
(1.5)(8.33)
=
12.5^\circ\mathrm{C}
$$

3. **F**

The result is only the temperature-rise magnitude associated with the simplified one-dimensional conduction region represented in the problem. It is not the complete temperature rise of a real component/PCB system.

---
