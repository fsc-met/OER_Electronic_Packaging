## Status

## Instructional Purpose

Make the Section 4.26 R17 troubleshooting case visually deterministic.

The reader should be able to compare four pieces of evidence:

1. **SPI:** Pad A = 63% of nominal; Pad B = 98% of nominal; supplied criterion = 80-120%;
2. **pre-reflow placement:** R17 centered, correct component and orientation;
3. **measured reflow evidence near R17:** peak = 241 °C; TAL = 66 s above 217 °C; both inside the supplied case window;
4. **post-reflow observation:** R17 tombstoned.

The figure should then visually guide the reader to:

> **Next investigation: printing**

without claiming that the SPI imbalance mathematically proves root cause.

The figure should reinforce the Section 4.16 troubleshooting model:

> **observation -> evidence -> likely mechanism -> upstream check -> next action**

---

## Central Teaching Message

> **The tombstone is the observation. The evidence determines where to investigate next.**

For this bounded case:

> **Unequal paste deposits are the strongest abnormal upstream evidence; placement is centered and the supplied nearby reflow peak/TAL are in window, so inspect the print process before changing the oven.**

A critical qualifier must remain visible or structurally obvious:

> **Strongest clue ≠ final proof. Verify the print-related cause.**

## Overall Layout

Use a left-to-right **four-evidence-panel chain** with a distinct conclusion region.

Recommended sequence:

### Panel A - SPI evidence

Show a simplified R17 two-pad footprint with two paste-deposit representations.

Labels:

- `Pad A: 63%`
- `Pad B: 98%`
- `Supplied criterion: 80-120%`

Show Pad A as outside the supplied criterion.

Show Pad B as inside the supplied criterion.

Use textual `FAIL` / `PASS` or symbols plus text. Do not rely on color alone.

### Panel B - Pre-reflow placement

Show R17 centered across the two pad/paste locations.

Required label:

- `R17 centered`
- `correct component / orientation`

Supporting conclusion:

- `No strong placement clue`

Do not imply perfect micrometer-level centering.

### Panel C - Reflow evidence near R17

Use a compact metric card rather than a full profile chart.

Required values:

- `Peak: 241 °C`
- `Supplied peak window: 235-245 °C`
- `TAL: 66 s above 217 °C`
- `Supplied TAL window: 45-90 s`

Required result:

- `within supplied case window`

Optional small note:

- `one supplied nearby measurement`

This note protects against implying that every board location is identical.

### Panel D - Post-reflow observation

Show a simplified 0603 resistor in a tombstoned condition:

- one end soldered to the pad;
- opposite end lifted.

Required labels:

- `R17 tombstoned`
- `Observation after reflow`

Do not label the defect as an oven failure.

### Conclusion / next-investigation region

Place below or to the right of the four evidence panels.

Required heading:

- `Best-supported next investigation`

Required main result:

- `PRINT PROCESS`

Required next checks, concise:

- `R17 stencil apertures`
- `paste release / clogging`
- `stencil underside / gasketing`
- `print alignment / board support`

Required qualifier:

- `Verify the cause before changing the process`

Recommended weak-action contrast:

- small muted crossed/secondary box:
  - `Do not change the oven first from this evidence`

Keep this subordinate to the positive next action.

## Required Objects and Labels

### 1. R17 / SPI panel

Required object:

- simplified pair of R17 pads;
- paste deposit on Pad A visibly smaller than Pad B.

Required text:

- `R17`
- `Pad A`
- `63%`
- `FAIL`
- `Pad B`
- `98%`
- `PASS`
- `supplied criterion 80-120%`

The percentages are **nominal paste volume**, not pad area.

Do not label 63% or 98% as transfer efficiency.

### 2. Placement panel

Required object:

- 0603 resistor centered over the two printed pads.

Required text:

- `Centered before reflow`
- `correct component`
- `correct orientation`

Optional small text:

- `no gross placement error observed`

Do not claim placement is statistically perfect.

### 3. Reflow evidence panel

Required text:

- `Peak 241 °C`
- `235-245 °C supplied window`
- `TAL 66 s`
- `45-90 s above 217 °C supplied window`
- `PASS for supplied case limits`

Optional small process icon:

- generic reflow profile trace or thermometer, provided it does not become a full chart.

The values must remain exactly as stated.

### 4. Tombstone panel

Required object:

- two pads;
- small chip resistor with one termination lifted;
- opposite termination soldered.

Required text:

- `Tombstone observed`

Optional small label:

- `post-reflow AOI`

Do not draw a solder bridge or open unrelated to the tombstone.

### 5. Evidence-to-decision arrows

Use arrows from all evidence panels toward the conclusion.

The visual weight should distinguish:

- **strong abnormal clue:** SPI imbalance;
- **evidence not supporting a gross placement cause:** centered placement;
- **evidence not supporting an oven-first response:** supplied nearby profile within window;
- **final observation:** tombstone.

The arrows should not suggest that the final tombstone causes the earlier SPI result.

### 6. Next-investigation box

Required main label:

- `Next investigation: PRINTING`

Required smaller labels:

- `stencil apertures`
- `paste release / clogging`
- `underside condition / gasketing`
- `print alignment / local support`

Required qualifier:

- `strongest upstream clue - verify before concluding root cause`

This box must not say `root cause = printing`.

## Required Technical Relationships

1. R17 Pad A has **63% of nominal paste volume**.
2. R17 Pad B has **98% of nominal paste volume**.
3. The supplied case criterion is **80-120% of nominal** for each R17 pad.
4. Pad A therefore fails the supplied case criterion.
5. Pad B therefore passes the supplied case criterion.
6. The numerical difference is **35 percentage points**, but the figure does not need to show that arithmetic unless space allows.
7. The placement evidence shows R17 centered before reflow with the correct component and orientation.
8. The placement evidence weakens a gross-placement-error explanation but does not prove placement contributed nothing.
9. The supplied nearby reflow evidence is:
   - peak 241 °C;
   - TAL 66 s above 217 °C.
10. The supplied case windows are:
   - peak 235-245 °C;
   - TAL 45-90 s above 217 °C.
11. The supplied nearby reflow evidence therefore passes those two supplied criteria.
12. A passing nearby measurement does **not** prove every board location is thermally identical.
13. Post-reflow AOI observes R17 tombstoned.
14. A tombstone reflects an imbalance in forces during solder melting/wetting; it is an observation, not a one-step root-cause diagnosis.
15. Unequal paste volume is a plausible contributor to unequal wetting-force development.
16. In this bounded case, the print imbalance is the strongest abnormal upstream evidence.
17. The correct next investigation is therefore the print process:
   - stencil apertures;
   - paste release/clogging;
   - stencil underside condition;
   - local gasketing/contact;
   - print alignment;
   - local board support.
18. The figure must **not** say that printing is proven as the root cause.
19. The figure must **not** recommend increasing/decreasing oven temperature, conveyor speed, or other reflow controls as the first action.
20. No universal SPI tolerance or universal SAC305 profile limit may be implied. All shown limits must be labeled as **supplied case** requirements.
21. The figure must not expand into formal MRB, CAPA, SPC, or Chapter 5 nonconformance handling.

## Spatial / Geometry Requirements

### Panel arrangement

- Use four aligned evidence panels of similar visual weight.
- Keep the numerical panels (SPI and reflow) wide enough for comfortable value labels.
- Use consistent panel heading positions.
- Use one arrow direction: left-to-right for evidence chronology.
- Use a separate downward/rightward decision region for the next investigation.

### SPI panel geometry

- Pad A and Pad B should be symmetrical in pad geometry.
- Paste deposits should differ visibly in **volume/amount representation**, not by changing the pad size.
- If top-view rectangles are used to represent deposits, label them as schematic so the viewer does not infer that top-view area alone is the volume measurement.
- Prefer a small volume bar or numeric badge alongside each pad to keep the 63/98 comparison unambiguous.

### Placement panel geometry

- Center the component over the two pads.
- Keep the component body horizontal.
- Both terminations should overlap the intended pad/paste regions approximately symmetrically.
- Do not show the lifted condition yet.

### Reflow panel geometry

- Use a compact metric card with two rows:
  - Peak;
  - TAL.
- Each row should pair measured value with supplied window.
- Use check symbols plus text.
- Do not create a detailed temperature-time curve unless it remains subordinate and technically consistent.

### Tombstone panel geometry

- Show one end clearly lifted.
- Keep one end visibly soldered/attached.
- Make the lifted angle large enough to read at 900 px but not cartoonishly vertical unless needed for clarity.
- Use a simple side/oblique schematic if it communicates the tombstone better than a top view.

### Decision region

- Give `PRINT PROCESS` the strongest emphasis after the evidence chain.
- Keep the `not an oven-first response` cue smaller and secondary.
- Route arrows so they do not pass through metric labels.
- Keep approximately **5-7% outer margins**.
- Maintain comfortable padding in every evidence card.
