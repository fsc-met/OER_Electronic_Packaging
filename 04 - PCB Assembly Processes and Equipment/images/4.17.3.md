# Figure 4.17.3 Suggested Content - Soldered Through-Hole Lead and Compliant Press-Fit Pin

This file defines the instructional content and figure-generation constraints for Figure 4.17.3 in Section 4.17, **Through-Hole Components and Insertion**.

The figure has one instructional role: **distinguish an ordinary soldered THT lead/pin from a compliant press-fit pin at the plated-hole interface**.

Do **not** call this specification or generated artwork canonical/final before explicit project-owner acceptance.

---

## Figure Title for Authoring / Reference Only

**Soldered Through-Hole Lead and Compliant Press-Fit Pin**

Do **not** place this overall title or the figure number inside the artwork.

---

## Instructional Purpose

Prevent a common conceptual error: treating every pin that enters a plated hole as the same type of interconnection.

The figure should answer:

> **Why can an ordinary THT lead enter a plated hole with clearance and require later soldering, while a compliant press-fit pin intentionally contacts the plated barrel and requires no solder joint?**

---

## Central Teaching Message

> **Soldered THT and press-fit both use plated holes, but the final interconnection mechanism is different: soldered THT relies on a later solder joint, while press-fit relies on controlled mechanical contact force between a compliant pin and the plated barrel.**

## Overall Layout

Use a clean **two-panel side-by-side cross-section** comparison.

### Left panel — conventional soldered THT
Show an ordinary straight lead/pin inserted through a plated component hole **before soldering**, with visible clearance between the lead and plated barrel. Include a small downward/next-step note: `Soldering follows`.

Add a **small, simplified `after soldering` cue** beneath the left panel: show solder wetting the lead/pad/barrel region without turning it into a workmanship/acceptance diagram. This resolves the filename/title wording while keeping the main left cross-section focused on pre-solder clearance.

### Right panel — compliant press-fit
Show a generic compliant pin section entering a specified plated hole and elastically deforming so that contact regions press against the plated barrel. Label `Installed press-fit` and `No solder joint required`.

Keep both PCB cross-sections at the same scale.

## Required Technical Relationships

1. The ordinary soldered THT lead must have visible clearance relative to the plated barrel before soldering.
2. The left panel must not imply that clearance itself is an electrical/mechanical final joint.
3. The press-fit pin must make intentional mechanical contact with the plated barrel.
4. The compliant section should visibly deform/compress during insertion; do not draw a rigid oversized pin simply gouging the hole.
5. Press-fit contact must occur at the plated barrel, not against exposed dielectric.
6. The plated wall must remain continuous in both panels.
7. Press-fit is a controlled pin-hole-plating system; do not imply an ordinary THT pin can be made press-fit merely by pushing harder.
8. Do not add universal press-fit insertion force, hole diameter/tolerance, plating thickness, or retention-force numbers.
9. Do not show molten solder, wave equipment, flux, or detailed solder-joint acceptance.
10. Do not imply that press-fit replaces soldered THT generally; it is an application-specific interconnection.

## Spatial / Geometry Requirements

- Use identical PCB thickness and comparable hole scales across the two panels.
- Keep the two PTH cross-sections vertically aligned.
- Exaggerate the ordinary-THT clearance slightly for teaching readability without making the hole look grossly oversized.
- Exaggerate compliant-pin deformation slightly so the contact mechanism is clear at 900 px.
- Keep the plated barrel thin but clearly visible and continuous.
- Use short leaders that terminate on the exact physical feature named.
- Keep the left-panel `Soldering follows` cue outside the main pre-solder PTH geometry so it cannot be mistaken for current solder.
- Keep the small `after soldering` cue visually secondary and clearly separated from the pre-solder comparison.
- In the press-fit panel, show **two opposed contact regions** where the compliant section bears against the barrel; do not depict 360-degree solid interference unless the chosen generic geometry genuinely supports it.
- Maintain approximately **6% outer margin** and comfortable panel padding.
- Avoid shading that makes the press-fit contact look like solder.

## Technical-Verification Basis

**Approved outline:** explicitly requires press-fit awareness within the THT insertion section.

**Current Section 4.17:** defines a soldered THT connection as a lead/pin inserted into a plated hole and later soldered, while a press-fit connection uses a specially designed compliant pin that deforms during insertion and maintains contact force against the plated barrel.

**Approved references:** Coombs supports compliant press-fit pin/PTH interaction and the need for controlled finished-hole/pin/plating conditions; current TE press-fit guidance supports the compliant-section/contact-force mechanism. Use these concepts for verification only; do not copy commercial compliant-pin geometry or vendor diagrams.
