## Instructional Purpose

Give a first-time Engineering Technology reader a direct top-view comparison of common pre-placement solder-paste deposit conditions.

The student should be able to visually distinguish:

1. a centered, separated, well-defined deposit;
2. an insufficient or incomplete deposit;
3. an excessive/smeared deposit that approaches or connects neighboring pads;
4. a deposit shifted away from the intended pad location.

The figure should support the practical question:

> **What visual evidence should make me stop and check the stencil, paste, support, alignment, cleanliness, or process before continuing production?**

---

## Central Teaching Message

> **Before placement and reflow, a useful solder-paste print is centered, separated, complete, and reasonably well defined. Too little, too much, smearing between pads, or misalignment are visible warning signs that should trigger investigation.**

Supporting idea:

- the figure teaches **recognition**, not final root-cause diagnosis or quantitative acceptance limits.

## Overall Layout

Use a **four-case top-view comparison**, preferably in one row if readability allows, otherwise a balanced 2×2 grid.

Recommended cases:

| Case | Visual condition |
|---|---|
| **Good / centered** | paste centered on intended pads, separated from neighbors, complete and well defined |
| **Too little / incomplete** | paste smaller, broken, irregular, or missing from part of pad |
| **Too much / connecting pads** | paste oversized, smeared, or visibly connecting neighboring pads |
| **Misaligned** | paste shifted away from the intended pad center/location |

Use the **same pad geometry and viewing scale** for all four cases so the student compares only the paste condition.

If two neighboring pads are used, keep their spacing identical in all cases.

## Required Objects and Labels

### Shared required objects

- PCB surface or neutral background plane;
- two or more representative PCB pads shown in top view;
- solder-paste deposits shown on/over the pads;
- short case labels.

### Case labels

Use concise labels such as:

- `Centered / well defined`
- `Too little / incomplete`
- `Too much / paste between pads`
- `Misaligned`

Avoid `PASS` / `FAIL` wording.

### Optional visual aids

- thin dashed outline showing the intended pad/deposit location;
- small arrow showing shift direction in the misalignment case;
- subtle callout to `paste between pads` in the excessive case.

Do not add quantitative SPI numbers.

---

## Required Technical Relationships

The final artwork must satisfy all of the following:

1. The **good** case is centered on the intended pad area and clearly separated from neighboring deposits.
2. The **too little** case visibly contains less or incomplete paste compared with the same intended geometry.
3. The **too much** case visibly extends beyond the intended deposit region and approaches or connects neighboring pads.
4. The **misaligned** case has roughly recognizable deposit shape but is shifted relative to the intended pad location.
5. The figure must represent conditions **before component placement and before reflow**.
6. The figure must not depict final solder fillets or completed joints.
7. The figure must not imply that appearance alone is sufficient for quantitative process acceptance.
8. The figure must not imply one universal dimensional acceptance tolerance.

---

## Spatial / Geometry Requirements

- Use identical pad size, pitch, and orientation across cases.
- Keep paste color/texture consistent across cases so only amount/location changes.
- Keep the good deposit clearly inside/over the intended pad geometry without making it unrealistically perfect.
- In the insufficient case, make the reduction or missing portion obvious at 900 px.
- In the excessive case, show paste encroachment or connection clearly without turning it into a post-reflow solder bridge.
- In the misaligned case, shift the paste enough to be educational but keep the intended pad visible.
- Maintain balanced internal padding around each panel.
- Leave **6–8% outer canvas margin**.
- No callout line may cross unrelated panels.

## Generation Guidance

Preferred workflow:

1. establish one clean pad/deposit geometry baseline;
2. duplicate the geometry consistently across all four cases;
3. change only paste amount/location for each condition;
4. add short labels;
5. inspect at 900 px;
6. freeze the visual baseline once the comparisons are technically clear;
7. make only targeted corrections thereafter.

If generated images introduce inconsistent pad geometry across cases, rebuild the figure as controlled vector artwork.
