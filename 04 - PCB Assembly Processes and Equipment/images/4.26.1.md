# Figure 4.26.1 Suggested Content - Integrated Case Product Map and Assembly Route

## Status

**Suggested-content specification - Rev1 QA-reviewed candidate**

This file defines the instructional content and generation constraints for Figure 4.26.1 in Section 4.26, **Integrated Factory-Floor Case: Plan, Build, and Troubleshoot**.

The figure has one clear instructional role:

> **Show the supplied product geometry and the selected physical assembly route for this specific mixed SMT/THT case.**

It must not become a generic route-family comparison, a troubleshooting diagram, or a detailed machine-anatomy figure.

Do **not** call this specification or generated artwork canonical/final before explicit project-owner acceptance.

**Integrated Case Product Map and Assembly Route**

## Instructional Purpose

Give the reader a compact visual map of the **Industrial Sensor Controller Rev B** before the worked decisions begin.

The figure should let a first-time Engineering Technology reader answer two questions visually:

1. **What is physically on the top and bottom sides of the board?**
2. **How does that product geometry lead to the selected manufacturing route?**

The visual should connect the case product to the route selected later in Section 4.26.4 without requiring the reader to mentally reconstruct the board from the BOM table.

The figure should also make the following case-specific planning logic visible:

- the bottom side carries only four small 0603 capacitors;
- the top side carries the main SMT population;
- the THT connector is inserted after both SMT reflow passes;
- the connector pins are then selectively soldered from the bottom side;
- final inspection follows the assembly operations.

---

## Central Teaching Message

> **Product geometry drives process sequence.**

For this specific case:

> **light bottom-side SMT first -> flip -> main top-side SMT second -> insert J1 -> selective solder -> inspect**

The figure must present this as **a reasonable route for the supplied case**, not as a universal rule for all double-sided or mixed-technology PCBAs.

A supporting message may be shown visually but should remain secondary:

> **The first-side SMT joints/components must remain acceptable through the second reflow pass.**

Do not imply that the first-side solder remains solid during the second pass.

## Required Objects and Labels

### 1. Top-side PCB map

Required heading:

- `TOP SIDE`

Required board label:

- `Industrial Sensor Controller - Rev B`

Required representative components:

- **R1-R20 / C1-C16** as grouped small 0603-passive population;
- **U1 - QFN-32**;
- **U2 - BGA-64**;
- **J1 - 2 x 8 THT connector body**.

The figure does not need to show every resistor and capacitor individually. Use a representative grouped population while keeping R17 recognizable if a label can be included without clutter.

Recommended labels:

- `U1  QFN-32`
- `U2  BGA-64`
- `J1  2 × 8 THT`
- `main SMT population`

Optional small label:

- `hidden joints under U2`

Do not show BGA solder balls as visible from the top-side view.

### 2. Bottom-side PCB map

Required heading:

- `BOTTOM SIDE`

Required components:

- `C17-C20`
- four small 0603 capacitor symbols only.

Required supporting cue:

- `light first-side SMT population`

The bottom view should not accidentally show U1, U2, or the body of J1 as bottom-mounted parts.

J1 plated holes/pin locations may be represented if needed to show where the connector pins later emerge through the board, but the connector body must remain clearly top-mounted.

### 3. Board orientation / two-sided relationship

Required visual relationship:

- top and bottom maps are the two sides of the **same board**;
- route begins with the bottom-side SMT population;
- board is flipped before the top-side SMT pass.

If a flip symbol is used, use a simple curved rotation/flip arrow and the label:

- `Flip board`

Do not use a 3D animation-style twist that makes component side/order ambiguous.

### 4. Bottom-side SMT route

Required steps:

- `Print`
- `SPI`
- `Place C17-C20`
- `Reflow 1`

Recommended phase label:

- `Bottom-side SMT pass`

The four bottom capacitors should be visually tied to this pass.

### 5. Top-side SMT route

Required steps:

- `Print`
- `SPI`
- `Place top-side SMT`
- `Reflow 2`

Recommended phase label:

- `Top-side SMT pass`

This pass includes U1, U2, and the main passive population.

Do not include J1 insertion before Reflow 2.

### 6. THT completion route

Required steps:

- `Insert J1`
- `Selective solder J1`
- `Required inspection`

Recommended visual:

- simplified connector insertion symbol;
- localized selective-solder nozzle/wave symbol aimed only at J1 pin region.

Do not show the whole underside immersed in a broad solder wave.

### 7. Small second-reflow awareness cue

Recommended supporting note near `Reflow 2`:

- `first-side passives remain on underside`

Optional second line:

- `same-alloy first-side joints may re-melt`

This cue is useful only if it can remain legible without crowding.

Do not portray re-melting as automatically defective.

## Required Technical Relationships

1. The top and bottom drawings must represent the **same PCB assembly**, not two products.
2. The bottom side contains only C17-C20 as the intentionally light SMT first-side population.
3. The top side contains the main SMT population: passives, U1 QFN, and U2 BGA.
4. J1 is a top-mounted THT connector inserted **after** both SMT reflow passes.
5. J1 pins extend through the PCB and are soldered from the bottom side.
6. The selected route is:
   - bottom-side print;
   - SPI;
   - placement;
   - Reflow 1;
   - flip;
   - top-side print;
   - SPI;
   - placement;
   - Reflow 2;
   - J1 insertion;
   - selective solder;
   - required inspection.
7. The first-side bottom 0603s remain on the underside during Reflow 2.
8. With the same alloy, first-side solder joints may re-melt during Reflow 2. The figure must not imply that they remain below liquidus.
9. The selected first-side population is light/small; the figure must not generalize this route to heavy or unsupported first-side components.
10. Selective soldering is chosen for this case because the released process plan confirms adequate nozzle access and local keep-out at J1.
11. The figure must not imply that selective soldering eliminates every thermal/access constraint.
12. The figure must not imply that wave soldering is technically impossible. It is simply not the selected route for this supplied case.
13. U2 is a hidden-joint package. A top-view outline may show U2 placement, but its solder joints must not be drawn as optically visible from above.
14. The final inspection block should remain generic. It may include `visual/AOI` and `X-ray when required`, but it must not teach Chapter 5 acceptance criteria.
15. No process parameter optimization, SPC, Cp/Cpk, MRB, MES, or formal quality-release workflow should appear.

## Spatial / Geometry Requirements

### Product-map region

- Keep the top-side and bottom-side boards aligned in orientation so the side relationship is easy to understand.
- Use approximately the same board outline for both views.
- Make the top-side population visibly denser than the bottom side.
- Keep U1, U2, and J1 separated enough that labels do not overlap.
- Use a simple rectangular PCB outline with rounded or slightly chamfered corners; no decorative perspective is required.
- Keep component silhouettes schematic and generic.
- If reference designators use leader lines, route them outside the component bodies and avoid line crossings.
- The bottom-side four-passive pattern should be unmistakable at 900 px.
- If J1 pin holes are shown on the bottom, they must align plausibly with the top-side J1 location.

### Route region

- Use one strong left-to-right process spine.
- Group route steps into three visible bands or bracketed regions:
  - bottom-side SMT;
  - top-side SMT;
  - THT completion.
- Give `Flip board` extra visual separation so students do not read both SMT passes as the same board side.
- Keep `Reflow 1` and `Reflow 2` distinct.
- Use normal engineering arrows that terminate clearly at the next process box.
- Do not route arrows through step labels.
- Do not allow the THT connector step to appear before Reflow 2.
- Place final inspection at the far right.
- Maintain approximately **5-7% outer margins**.

### Text fit

- Use intentional two-line wrapping where needed.
- Keep route-step text shorter than explanatory prose.
- No text may touch box borders.
- Do not solve crowding mainly by shrinking text.
