## Figure Title for Authoring / Reference Only

**Equal Footprints Can Have Different Paste Volumes**

Do **not** place this overall title or the figure number inside the artwork.

---

## Instructional Purpose

Show a first-time Engineering Technology reader that two solder-paste deposits can appear to have the **same top-view area** while containing substantially different amounts of paste because their height profiles differ.

The figure should make the Section 4.9 worked comparison visual:

- both deposits have the same simplified footprint area: **0.20 mm²**;
- Deposit A has representative mean height: **100 µm = 0.100 mm**;
- Deposit B has representative mean height: **60 µm = 0.060 mm**;
- simplified approximate volumes:
  - A: **0.020 mm³**;
  - B: **0.012 mm³**;
- B therefore contains **40% less volume** than A in this simplified example.

The figure should answer:

> **Why can a deposit look acceptable from above but still contain too little paste?**

---

## Central Teaching Message

> **Top-view footprint area alone does not determine paste volume. Two deposits can have the same footprint while different height profiles produce different three-dimensional volumes.**

Supporting ideas:

- this is a simplified teaching calculation;
- the example uses **footprint area × mean height**;
- a real 3D SPI system determines volume from the measured height distribution, not from a single peak-height number.

## Overall Layout

Use a **two-column comparison**.

### Left column - Deposit A

Show:

1. top view with a simple rectangular/rounded footprint labeled:
   - `Area = 0.20 mm²`
2. directly below, a side view of the same footprint with a taller paste profile:
   - `Mean height = 100 µm = 0.100 mm`
3. concise result:
   - `Approx. volume = 0.020 mm³`

### Right column - Deposit B

Show:

1. top view with the **same footprint size and shape**:
   - `Area = 0.20 mm²`
2. directly below, a lower paste profile:
   - `Mean height = 60 µm = 0.060 mm`
3. concise result:
   - `Approx. volume = 0.012 mm³`

### Bottom comparison statement

Use one concise statement:

`Same top-view area, different height -> different volume`

Optional small note:

`Deposit B contains 40% less paste volume in this simplified example.`

A small footnote-style note may state:

`Teaching approximation: footprint area × mean height. Real 3D SPI uses the measured height distribution.`

## Required Technical Relationships

The final artwork must satisfy all of the following:

1. The two top-view footprints must be **identical in visible area and shape**.
2. Deposit A must visibly have the greater representative height.
3. Deposit B must visibly have the lower representative height.
4. The values must satisfy:
   - $0.20\ \text{mm}^2 \times 0.100\ \text{mm} = 0.020\ \text{mm}^3$;
   - $0.20\ \text{mm}^2 \times 0.060\ \text{mm} = 0.012\ \text{mm}^3$.
5. The figure must correctly show that 0.012 mm³ is 40% lower than 0.020 mm³.
6. The calculation must be described as a simplified approximation using **mean height**, not peak height.
7. The figure must not imply that all real deposits are flat-topped rectangular prisms.
8. The figure must not imply that a real SPI system uses only one height sample.
9. No pass/fail threshold should be shown.
