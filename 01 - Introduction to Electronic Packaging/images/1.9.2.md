## Suggested Content for Figure 1.9.2

**Instructional purpose:**  
Show the physical construction of a representative **wire-bond IC package** and explain how a face-up die is connected to the package interconnect structure using fine metal wires.

The main teaching message is:

> **face-up die -> die pads -> wire bonds -> package bond fingers / leads -> external terminals**

The figure should help readers distinguish wire bonds from PCB traces and understand the role of die attach, leadframe/package substrate, encapsulation, and external package terminals.

**Filename:**  
`1.9.2 - Wire Bond IC Package Construction.png`

**Intended width:** 900 px in Markdown

**Recommended aspect ratio:** 16:9

---

### Overall layout

Use one clean conceptual package cross-section or cutaway view.

Preferred structure:

- package body / mold compound around the assembly;
- face-up semiconductor die near center;
- die attach beneath the die;
- leadframe or package substrate beneath/around die;
- fine wire loops from die pads to package bond fingers;
- external package leads / terminals.

Do not embed the external figure title or caption.

---

### Required objects

#### Face-up semiconductor die

Show:

- active surface facing upward;
- several visible die bond pads near perimeter;
- die physically attached to support below.

Required label:

- `Face-up die`

Optional label:

- `Die bond pads`

#### Die attach

Show a thin layer beneath the die.

Required label:

- `Die attach`

Optional note:

- `Mechanical / thermal attachment`

Do not imply one universal die-attach material.

#### Leadframe or package substrate

Choose one representative support structure.

For introductory clarity, a **leadframe-based molded package** is preferred.

Required label:

- `Leadframe`

Optional labels:

- `Die paddle`
- `Bond fingers`

If a substrate-based version is used instead, label it clearly as `Package substrate`.

Do not mix leadframe and multilayer substrate features in a way that makes the construction ambiguous.

#### Wire bonds

Show several fine curved wire loops.

Required label:

- `Wire bonds`

The wires must connect:

- one end to die bond pads;
- the other end to leadframe bond fingers / substrate pads.

Do not let wires float.

Do not draw them like PCB traces.

#### Mold compound / protective body

Show a surrounding protective molded body.

Required label:

- `Mold compound`

Optional note:

- `Protects die and wire bonds`

Do not imply the molded body is hermetic.

#### External package terminals

Show visible external leads or lands.

Required label:

- `External leads`

Preferred package visual:

- SOIC / QFP-like leaded surface-mount package;
- or a generic leadframe package with clear gull-wing leads.

---

### Required technical relationships

The figure must show correctly that:

- the die is face-up;
- the die is mechanically attached before wire bonding;
- wire bonds connect die pads to leadframe/substrate bond locations;
- the package body protects but does not perform the semiconductor circuit function;
- external package terminals connect the package to the next assembly level;
- the wire-bond path is longer than a direct flip-chip bump path;
- package geometry is conceptual, not a universal construction.

### Prohibited or misleading elements

Do not:

- show a face-down die;
- show bumps under the die as the primary interconnect;
- show wire bonds connecting directly to PCB traces;
- show wire bonds floating;
- show package body before the internal connections are visually understood;
- imply every wire-bond package uses gold wire;
- imply every wire-bond package has the same lead geometry.
