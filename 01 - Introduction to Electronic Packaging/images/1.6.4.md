## Suggested Content for Figure 1.6.4

**Figure Title:** CMOS Realization of a Two-Input NAND Gate

**Purpose:**  
Show why a CMOS NAND gate uses parallel PMOS devices and series NMOS devices.

**Suggested Content:**  
Create a clear transistor-level two-input CMOS NAND circuit.

Required elements:

- $V_{DD}$ at the top;
- two PMOS transistors in **parallel** in the pull-up network;
- two NMOS transistors in **series** in the pull-down network;
- inputs $A$ and $B$ clearly connected to the appropriate PMOS/NMOS gate pairs;
- output $Y$ clearly shown between the pull-up and pull-down networks;
- GND/reference at the bottom;
- same NMOS and PMOS symbol style already used in this book.

Include a short visual explanation:

- if either input is 0, a PMOS pull-up path can exist;
- only when both inputs are 1 does the full NMOS series pull-down path conduct;
- therefore $Y=0$ only for $A=B=1$.

Do not show every possible input state as a separate full circuit unless readability remains excellent.

**Style Requirements:**
- one gate per figure;
- white background;
- large, readable transistor symbols;
- very clear input, output, $V_{DD}$, and GND labels;
- restrained highlighting of conducting paths;
- no embedded figure number or overall title.
