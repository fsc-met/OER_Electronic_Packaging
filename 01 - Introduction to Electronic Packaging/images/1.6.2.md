## Suggested Content for Figure 1.6.2

**Instructional purpose:**  
Show how complementary NMOS and PMOS switching produces the simplest CMOS logic function.

**Filename:**  
`1.6.2 - NMOS and PMOS Forming a CMOS Inverter.png`

**Intended width:** 900 px

**Recommended aspect ratio:** 16:9

### Layout

Use two matched side-by-side circuit panels:

1. `Logic 0 input → Logic 1 output`
2. `Logic 1 input → Logic 0 output`

Use controlled conventional MOSFET symbols. Do not embed an overall title or figure number.

### Required topology

Each panel must show:

- $V_{DD}$ at top;
- PMOS upper pull-up device;
- NMOS lower pull-down device;
- both gates connected to one input;
- both drains joined at the output;
- NMOS source toward GND/circuit reference.

### Logic-low input state

Show:

- PMOS `ON`;
- NMOS `OFF`;
- pull-up path active;
- output `Logic 1`.

### Logic-high input state

Show:

- PMOS `OFF`;
- NMOS `ON`;
- pull-down path active;
- output `Logic 0`.

### Important technical note

This is the **idealized stable-state model**. Do not say real CMOS consumes zero power. Real circuits have leakage and switching power.

### Prohibited or misleading elements

- Do not reverse PMOS and NMOS.
- Do not show both devices ON or both OFF in either stable-state example.
- Do not assign universal numerical logic voltages.
- Do not mix inconsistent MOSFET symbol conventions.
