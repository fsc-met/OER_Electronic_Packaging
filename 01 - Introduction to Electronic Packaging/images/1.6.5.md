## Suggested Content for Figure 1.6.5

**Figure Title:** CMOS Realization of a Two-Input NOR Gate

**Purpose:**  
Show why a CMOS NOR gate uses series PMOS devices and parallel NMOS devices.

**Suggested Content:**  
Create a clear transistor-level two-input CMOS NOR circuit.

Required elements:

- $V_{DD}$ at the top;
- two PMOS transistors in **series** in the pull-up network;
- two NMOS transistors in **parallel** in the pull-down network;
- inputs $A$ and $B$ clearly connected to the appropriate PMOS/NMOS gate pairs;
- output $Y$ clearly shown between the pull-up and pull-down networks;
- GND/reference at the bottom;
- same NMOS and PMOS symbol style already used in this book.

Include a short visual explanation:

- only when both inputs are 0 can the complete PMOS series pull-up path conduct;
- if either input is 1, an NMOS pull-down branch can conduct;
- therefore $Y=1$ only for $A=B=0$.

**Style Requirements:**
- one gate per figure;
- white background;
- large, readable transistor symbols;
- clear series/parallel structure;
- no embedded figure number or overall title.
