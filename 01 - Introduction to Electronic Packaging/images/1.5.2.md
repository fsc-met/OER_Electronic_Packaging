## Suggested Content for Figure 1.5.2

**Instructional purpose:**  
Show one representative way capacitance can be created within an IC using a MOS structure. The figure should help students recognize that an integrated capacitor is a fabricated conductor-dielectric-semiconductor structure rather than a tiny packaged capacitor placed inside the die.

**Filename:**  
`1.5.2 - Example of an Integrated MOS Capacitor.png`

**Intended width:** 900 px in Markdown

**Recommended aspect ratio:** 16:9

### Representative structure

Show a clean conceptual cross-section of a **MOS capacitor**.

Required elements:

- conductive gate electrode;
- thin gate dielectric / oxide;
- semiconductor body or substrate;
- gate terminal;
- body/substrate terminal;
- charge or electric-field indication across the dielectric.

Suggested labels:

- `Gate electrode`
- `Gate dielectric (oxide)`
- `Semiconductor body`
- `Gate terminal`
- `Body / substrate terminal`
- `Electric field`

### Required concept

The figure should communicate:

`Conductive gate + dielectric + semiconductor region → capacitance`

The gate must remain physically separated from the semiconductor by the dielectric.

### Important scope note

This is **one representative integrated-capacitor structure**.

Do not imply that all integrated capacitors are MOS capacitors. MIM capacitors, junction capacitors, and other process-specific structures also exist.

### Visual style

- simple textbook cross-section;
- white or very light background;
- clear conductor/dielectric/semiconductor distinction;
- restrained charge or electric-field arrows;
- large readable labels;
- no photorealism;
- no embedded figure number or overall title.

### Prohibited or misleading elements

- Do not show current flowing normally through the intact oxide.
- Do not confuse the capacitor with a complete MOSFET by adding unnecessary source and drain regions.
- Do not show photons or photodetector behavior.
- Do not claim that MOS capacitance is independent of bias.
- Do not assign one universal dielectric thickness or capacitance value.

### Technical verification sources

- Current MET406 Section 1.5 draft.
- Rao R. Tummala, *Fundamentals of Microsystems Packaging*, used for integrated-passive context.
- Current MET406 OER project and image-generation rules.

### Copyright/originality note

### Final caption

*Figure 1.5.2 – A MOS capacitor is one example of an integrated capacitor. A conductive gate and the semiconductor region are separated by a thin dielectric, allowing electrical charge and energy to be stored in the resulting electric field.*

Describe a MOS capacitor cross-section showing a gate electrode, thin oxide dielectric, semiconductor body, terminals, and an electric field across the dielectric.
