## Suggested Content for Figure 1.6.1

**Instructional purpose:**  
Help students distinguish analog, digital, and mixed-signal circuit functions without introducing detailed transistor-level circuit design.

**Filename:**  
`1.6.1 - Analog Digital and Mixed-Signal Circuit Functions.png`

**Intended width:** 900 px

**Recommended aspect ratio:** 16:9

### Layout

Use three matched side-by-side panels:

1. **Analog**
2. **Digital**
3. **Mixed-Signal**

Do not embed an overall title or figure number inside the artwork.

### Analog panel

Show:

- a generic continuously varying input waveform;
- a simple block labeled `Analog circuit`;
- a continuously varying output waveform.

Do not imply that analog signals must be sinusoidal.

### Digital panel

Show:

- a realistic digital-style waveform with finite rise/fall transitions;
- `Logic 0 range`;
- `Logic 1 range`;
- a block labeled `Digital logic`.

Do not assign universal numerical voltage values.

### Mixed-signal panel

Show a conceptual flow such as:

`Analog input → ADC → Digital processing → DAC / output → Analog output`

### Required technical relationships

- Analog signals vary continuously.
- Digital circuits interpret voltage ranges as discrete states.
- Mixed-signal circuits include an actual analog/digital interface or conversion function.

### Visual style

- clean engineering-textbook infographic;
- flat 2D presentation;
- restrained colors;
- large readable labels;
- white/light background;
- no decorative icons;
- no embedded overall title.

### Prohibited or misleading elements

- Do not use fixed universal logic voltages.
- Do not show mathematically instantaneous digital transitions.
- Do not imply analog means only sine waves.
- Do not show detailed ADC/DAC architectures.

### Verification sources

- MET406 Chapter 1 lecture notes and prior Chapter 1 draft.
- Current MET406 Sections 1.4 and 1.5.
- Current MET406 OER project and image-generation rules.

### Final caption

*Figure 1.6.1 – Analog circuits process continuously varying electrical quantities, digital circuits interpret voltage ranges as discrete logic states, and mixed-signal circuits combine both types of functions.*

### Alt-text intent

Describe three panels showing analog, digital, and mixed-signal circuit behavior.
