Minor editorial corrections, figure refinements, formatting changes, and internal development changes may not be listed individually.

The book is under active development. Later entries should be added when chapters or published editions reach significant milestones.

## August 31, 2026

  - **1.19 Practice Problems**

- Adopted the same **Chapter Summary -> Practice Problems -> Practice Problem Keys** structure for future chapters.
- Revised the project outline to reflect the new chapter-end structure.
- Prepared project-level Credits and Acknowledgments, Disclaimer, License, and Revision History files for the mdBook/OpenEngineeringBooks publication workflow.
